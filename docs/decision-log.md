## Autonomy roadmap and entry criteria

Formalised in ADR-0008.

Autonomy grows in phases. A phase starts only when The Ledger shows its entry criteria are met, and the switch is made by a PR to The Charter approved by Krzysztof. Thresholds below are initial proposals, to be tuned with real data.

| Phase | What is enabled | Entry criteria |
|-------|-----------------|----------------|
| 0 — Supervised (start) | Local agents only, under Krzysztof's account with `Agent:` trailer. Every PR reviewed and merged by a human. | Skeleton, Wardens (scope guard, command guard, Definition of Done), CI gates and branch ruleset in place. |
| 1 — Assisted | OVERSEER plans and splits tasks; implementer/reviewer roles with model routing and escalation; Sentinel review on every PR. Still human merge. | At least 20 agent PRs merged; CI first-pass green rate at least 70%; no Warden bypass found; Ledger dashboard live. |
| 2 — Night shift | Dedicated **GitHub App** identity for cloud/autonomous agents; scheduled overnight runs (Nightwatch, implementation of `agent:ready` issues); per-task and per-night budgets; kill switch. Human merge for everything. | Phase 1 for at least 4 weeks; at least 50 agent PRs; human rework rate under 20%; zero high-severity security findings from agent code; budget alerts tested. |
| 3 — Auto-merge low risk | Auto-merge to staging for `risk:low` PRs with all gates green (incl. Sentinel, bug-reproduction test, coverage on changed lines); auto-revert on failed post-deploy checks. Medium risk: one-click approval. High risk and production: always human. | Phase 2 for at least 4 weeks; revert rate of agent PRs under 5%; Infiltrator red-team runs active with no open critical findings. |
